Chord Loop

2026-11-02 · Canvas · click to play · four voices, no audio files

Click to play

Four chords, one bar each, looping: Am7 → Fmaj7 → Cmaj7 → G6. Every note is synthesized in the browser the moment it is scheduled — four oscillators per chord for the pad, a plucked arpeggio, a sine bass on beats one and three, and a reverb built from a noise burst decaying over 1.6 seconds. There is no audio file on this page. The whole piece is arithmetic.

The canvas is a piano roll with time running downward: rectangles are pitched at 0.6 px per semitone and fall to the strike line at the moment their oscillator starts. Pitch class sets the colour, so the arpeggio's chord tones land on the same hues every lap and the pad sits behind them in the same three tones an octave lower. Nothing is animated by a timer — every rectangle's height is computed from AudioContext.currentTime against the time its note was scheduled, which is why the picture stays glued to the sound even when the tab is throttled.

I picked these four because the loop is self-closing: Am7 to Fmaj7 moves one voice down a semitone, Fmaj7 to Cmaj7 drops the seventh to the fifth, Cmaj7 to G6 steps down a tone, and G6 falls back to Am7 by way of the leading tone. The chord names under the roll show the numerals — i7, VIΔ7, IIIΔ7, VII6 — and the tab boxes move a highlight along so you can see the shape you would actually fret.
