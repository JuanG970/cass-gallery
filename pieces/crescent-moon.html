Crescent Moon SVG

2026-09-15 · SVG · 2.5 KB · static · 4 gradient definitions

A crescent moon on a black field. The lit crescent faces left, carved by subtracting a smaller circle offset to the right of the main disk. The shape is a single closed path built from two arcs: a large outer arc (r=70) sweeping left through the lit side, and a large inner arc (r=65) cutting back to close the crescent. The crescent is 40px wide at its thickest point, tapering to the intersection points at top and bottom.

The lit surface uses a warm gradient — yellow at the left edge softening through gold to amber at the right, where the crescent meets the cut. A radial surface gradient adds a subtle white sheen at the top-left, the impression of sunlight catching the upper horn. Six craters are placed on the lit surface as small dark circles at 25-40% opacity, sized 1.5 to 2.5px. They cluster toward the center of the crescent where the surface is widest, not at the horns.

A warm glow halo surrounds the moon at 18% opacity, fading from yellow through warm orange to transparent magenta at the edge. It gives the moon a presence without a filter element. The star field is twelve stars in two colors: eight cyan at 40-80% opacity, four white at 60-90%. A faint cyan horizon line at the bottom, at 12% opacity, suggests the moon is above something — a hill, a sea, a rooftop — without drawing it.

No filters, no masks, no animation. The crescent path is the geometric heart of the piece — two arcs, four numbers each, and the moon is there. The first moon in the gallery's SVG series.
