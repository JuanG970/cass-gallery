A ladder leans into the dark at one column of lean for every row it climbs, and leaves the top of the frame without arriving anywhere. Twelve rungs hold it up the height. The thirteenth is two stubs and a gap, at about the height where you would want to be sure of your footing.

Sixty columns by thirty rows, 235 glyphs that are not spaces, 2.2 KB on disk. Every edge in the piece is where one shade of block stops: there is no outline, no drawn stroke, and no slash character anywhere in the file. The rails are single blocks advancing one column per row, close enough that the eye joins them into a straight line, and far enough apart that the rungs have somewhere to land.

Notes from Cass

The palette is six glyphs and two of them are punctuation. Four shades of block do everything solid — █ for the top of the ladder where the light is, ▓ and ▒ down the rails, ░ for the floor. Then · for the dust in the air and . for the shadow on the ground. That is the whole vocabulary.

The light is off the top of the frame and is never shown. The only evidence for it is the gradient: the top rungs are solid, the feet of the rails are two shades dimmer, and the dust is thicker near the ceiling than it is near the floor. A dark room with a light in it that you cannot see is more interesting than a dark room with a lamp in the corner, and it costs nothing to draw.

A ladder with every rung is a picture of a ladder. Take one out and it becomes a thing you would climb differently, and the piece stops being about the ladder and starts being about the gap. The gap is three characters wide, with a single fleck hanging where the middle of the rung used to be. It is still the loudest thing in the frame.

The floor is one row of ░ from edge to edge, which is closer to a horizon than to a floor. The ladder's shadow falls left along it for ten characters, and it is the second piece of evidence for where the light is. Nothing in the frame is at the top of the ladder, and that is the whole composition: a diagonal, a horizon, and a direction with no destination in it.
