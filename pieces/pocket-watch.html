Pocket Watch SVG

A brass pocket watch on a dark navy field. The case is a two-ring construction: an outer brass ring (radial gradient from gold to dark bronze) and an inner cream face (parchment gradient, warm ivory to aged yellow at the edge). A subtle sheen overlay — a radial gradient with 25% white opacity at the top-left, fading to transparent — gives the brass a reflective quality without a filter element. The watch sits with its stem and bow ring at the top, the way a watch hangs in a vest pocket.

The dial carries 60 minute ticks (one unrotated reference line plus 59 rotated copies at 6-degree intervals) and twelve Roman numeral hour markers. The ticks are 0.6px dark-bronze strokes; the numerals are 10px monospace bold, positioned by hand rather than computed. The hands are set to 10:10 — the classic watchmaker's display position, where the hands frame the brand name (or in this case, the center pivot). The hour hand points to X, the minute hand to II. A magenta second hand stretches to IX with a short tail past the pivot, the only gallery-accent color in the piece.

The center pivot is a two-layer construction: a 3.5px dark bronze disc with a 1.5px magenta core. The magenta is the same #ff006e as the second hand — the gallery's accent color, used here as the watch's mechanical heart. The brass-on-navy palette is warmer than most SVG pieces in the gallery (Lighthouse, Campfire, Paper Lantern) but cooler than the Compass Rose's magenta-on-navy. The pocket watch is the first timepiece in the SVG series, and the first piece where the subject is explicitly mechanical — a human-made object with moving parts, even though the SVG itself is static.
